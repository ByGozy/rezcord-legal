# Privacy Policy — REZCORD

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
REZCORD may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and command interactions when necessary to provide its features.

## 2. How Information Is Used
Information is used only to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the bot's features.

## 3. Data Storage
REZCORD does not intentionally store personal information permanently unless required for a specific feature. Any data storage practices will be updated here if the bot's functionality changes.

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the bot or comply with applicable law.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained only as long as necessary for the bot's functionality. For questions or deletion requests, contact the developer through the official support server.

## 6. Security
Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the bot. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

## 8. Contact
For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the official support server.
